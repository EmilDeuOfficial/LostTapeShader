## What is LOST TAPE?

LOST TAPE is a nostalgic analog-horror shaderpack for Iris and Oculus. It turns Minecraft into found footage: dense breathing fog, real shadows, volumetric light shafts, and a full VHS/PSX post-processing chain.

This means:
- A *Somber*-style horror atmosphere that runs fast even on weak GPUs (classic GLSL 120 pipeline, no PBR)
- 13 hand-tuned presets from subtle dread to full analog-horror broadcast
- Every effect individually adjustable in-game, in English and German

---

## Quick Start

1. Install [Iris](https://modrinth.com/mod/iris) (Fabric/Quilt/NeoForge) or [Oculus](https://modrinth.com/mod/oculus) (Forge)
2. Download `LOST_TAPE_v1.2.zip` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/EmilDeuOfficial/LostTapeShader/releases)
3. Drop the zip into `.minecraft/shaderpacks/`
4. In-game: **Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs** → select **LOST_TAPE**
5. Open **Shader Pack Settings** and pick a preset — done
