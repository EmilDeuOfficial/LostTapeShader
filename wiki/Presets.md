# Presets

LOST TAPE ships 13 hand-tuned presets. Select them with the profile button at the top of **Shader Pack Settings**.

Every preset sets **all** options — one click always produces a complete, consistent look with no leftovers from the previous preset.

---

## Overview

| Preset | Mood | Standout traits |
|---|---|---|
| **Lost Tape** (default) | Balanced analog horror | Fog, sickly green tint, grain, light PSX wobble |
| **Director's Cut** | Dark & heavy | Dense fog, fully swallowed sky, scanlines |
| **Verity** | Grim, glitchy | Very dark, crushed blacks, VHS glitch, strong shadows |
| **Backrooms** | Wrong & washed out | Yellow tint, faded blacks, fluorescent flicker, soft flat shadows |
| **VHS Tape** | Noisy cassette | Scanlines, wobble, sepia, strong chromatic aberration |
| **Camcorder '98** | Found footage | Clear-ish shaky image, brighter nights, heavy vignette |
| **PSX Horror** | PS1 survival horror | Pixelation, 16 colors, hard shadows, fog at the camera |
| **Silent Fog** | Silent-Hill | White-grey total fog, very soft shadows, intense god rays |
| **Dead Channel** | Broadcast dread | Cold blue, deep dark, heavy vignette and grain |
| **Old Film** | Pure nostalgia | Strong sepia, desaturated, heavy grain, soft light |
| **Sunless** | ARG endgame | No sun, no moon — but the light shafts keep falling |
| **Retro Arcade** | Fun, no horror | Full colors, pixels + dithering, barely any fog |
| **Faint Signal** | Subtle | Light dread for normal survival play |

---

## Preset design notes

- **Verity** and **Dead Channel** raise torch brightness to 1.5 so light sources punch through the darkness.
- **PSX Horror** is the only preset with vertex snapping on mobs — authentic PS1 jitter. Turn off *Snap Mobs/Entities* under **Retro / PSX** if modded round entities look deformed.
- **Sunless** hides both sun and moon while keeping volumetric light at full strength with maximum shadow-streak contrast — light with no source.
- **Retro Arcade** and **Faint Signal** are meant for actually playing the game; everything else prioritizes atmosphere.

---

## Making your own preset

1. Start from the preset closest to what you want
2. Adjust options — see [Settings](Settings)
3. Iris saves your tweaks automatically to `shaderpacks/LOST_TAPE_v1.2.zip.txt`

Back up that `.txt` to keep your custom look across reinstalls.
